# Zahir Haynes-Sharif

I am an independent undergraduate researcher and project lead on Infinity. My current research question is:

**Can persistent structured epistemic state improve long-horizon evidence tracking and belief revision relative to strong alternative memory representations under controlled conditions?**

My research interests include AI systems, LLM evaluation, AI agents, persistent memory and epistemic state, networking, distributed systems, and reproducible experimentation.

## Current research

[Public Infinity research overview](https://github.com/immmortalitized/infinity-research-overview)

Infinity remains in engineering and methodological validation. INF-EXP-0001 is a planned controlled comparison of memory representations for evidence tracking and belief revision. No empirical H2 claim has been made.

Real-model testing is intentionally blocked until evaluator isolation, comparator fairness, and methodological gates are satisfied. These gates are not represented as having passed.

A future positive result would apply only to the tested bundled structured-state treatment. It would not establish adaptive orchestration, substrate independence, self-improvement, or the broader Infinity thesis.

I value negative results, strong controls, fair comparators, versioned methodology, and deliberate attempts to falsify the hypothesis. These are research commitments and plans, not claims of completed validation.
